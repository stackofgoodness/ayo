How to Play Ayò

  1. Each player starts with four seeds in each of their six holes.
  2. Choose one of your holes to pick up all its seeds.
  3. Move around the board, dropping one seed into each hole in turn.
  4. When a hole reaches exactly four seeds, those seeds may be captured according to the game's capture rules.
  5. Your captured seeds are added to your seed jar.
  6. When a round ends, the player with the most captured seeds wins the round.
