Click one of your holes. All seeds are picked up, then dropped one by one clockwise. If the final seed lands in a hole that already has seeds, all seeds in that hole are picked up and sown again.

How to play Ayò

  1. Each player starts with six holes containing four seeds each.
  2. On your turn, choose a non-empty hole on your side.
  3. All seeds in that hole are picked up and the hole becomes empty.
  4. Move clockwise, dropping one seed in each following hole.
  5. If the final seed lands in an occupied hole, pick up every seed in that hole and continue sowing.
  6. Your turn ends when the final seed lands in an empty hole.
  7. If that final seed lands alone in an empty hole on your side and the opposite hole contains seeds, you capture both groups.
  8. If a player has no seeds, the game ends. The other player collects the remaining seeds.
  9. The player with more captured seeds wins. A 24–24 result is a draw.
